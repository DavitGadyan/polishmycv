Senior Machine Learning Engineer (LLM / Agents) — Remote
Meridian Health Technologies

About the role
We're building AI copilots for insurance underwriting and patient onboarding. You'll own production LLM systems end to end: RAG pipelines, agentic workflows with tool use, evaluation, and deployment on AWS with Kubernetes.

What you'll do
- Design and ship LLM-powered features (RAG, agents, structured extraction from documents such as insurance cards and IDs)
- Build and maintain FastAPI services, vector stores and retrieval quality evals
- Fine-tune and serve open-source models when hosted APIs aren't suitable
- Work under HIPAA constraints with PHI; partner with compliance
- Deploy with Docker/Kubernetes on AWS; monitoring and cost control
- Collaborate with product, clinicians and brokers; communicate trade-offs clearly

Must have
- 5+ years in ML engineering or data science, 2+ shipping LLM applications in production
- Python, FastAPI, LangChain or LangGraph, vector databases (Pinecone/pgvector)
- Docker, Kubernetes, AWS
- Strong SQL (PostgreSQL) and data pipeline experience
- Experience with OCR / document extraction is a plus
- Healthcare or insurance domain experience is a strong plus
